# CaseNoter

CaseNoter now opens as its own Windows app.

**If you use an older version, you need to install 3.0 once. After that, updates are automatic.**

## Install

1. [Download CaseNoter](https://github.com/GearUnclear/casenoter-dist/releases/latest/download/CaseNoter-Setup.exe).
2. Open the downloaded file, **CaseNoter-Setup.exe**.
3. Follow the instructions. Leave the folder as shown and choose **Housing** or **Education & Employment (EE&T)**.
4. Choose the desktop shortcut option, finish installing, and open **CaseNoter**.

Stay connected to the internet during installation. If you get stuck, contact support.

## Already use CaseNoter?

Bring your saved setup into the new app:

1. Open your old CaseNoter and unlock it. Go to **Settings → Export my setup**. Save the file somewhere easy to find, such as **Downloads**.
2. In the new app, choose **Move my existing setup** and select the file you just saved.
3. Enter your **existing CaseNoter password**, then choose **Import and open CaseNoter**.

Keep your old CaseNoter until you have checked that everything works in the new app.

**New to CaseNoter?** Follow the setup instructions in the app instead.

## Opening CaseNoter next time

Double-click the **CaseNoter** shortcut on your desktop. Finish saving your work before closing the app.
